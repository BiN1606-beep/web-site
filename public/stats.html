      What the numbers mean

      Active
      Someone did a daily check-in or finished a step that day. Just opening the site does not count.
      Week 2 return rate
      Of the people who joined 2 to 6 weeks ago, how many were still active in their second week. Above 30% is good for a habit app.
      Stickiness
      Active today divided by active this month. 20% or more means people come back often.
      At the goal limit
      People who use all their goal slots. A sign of who might pay for Osiris Plus one day.
      AI requests
      Calls to the free Groq AI. Watch this to stay inside the free limit.
